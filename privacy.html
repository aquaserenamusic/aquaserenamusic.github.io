Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy describes how the application Aqua Serena Uploader (“the app”), operated by Aqua Serena Music, uses data obtained through the YouTube API Services.

What the app does

The app is an internal tool used only by the owner of the Aqua Serena Music YouTube channel to upload the channel's own original videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, and manage the channel's own playlists. It is not offered to the public and no other user signs in to it.

Data accessed

The app accesses only the Aqua Serena Music channel, through an OAuth authorization granted by the channel owner. It does not access, collect, store or share personal data of any YouTube viewer or any other user. It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Storage and security

The authorization token is stored encrypted as a secret in a private, access-controlled repository and is used only to perform the actions above. No data obtained from the YouTube API Services is sold or shared with third parties.

Third-party terms

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account › Third-party connections.
